The first time you open LUCID, it asks a few short questions, one at a time. Every question can be skipped, and you can change every answer later. This page walks through them in order.

## Pick your role

The first box is titled **Welcome - what's your role?** Your role decides what LUCID shows you first. It never locks anything away: every panel stays one keyboard shortcut away, **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux or **⌘K** on a Mac.

<Shot
  id="get-started-role-picker"
  alt="The Welcome - what's your role? box with five cards: Developer, Security engineer, Manager, Executive and LUCID Agent, and a Skip - use Developer button at the bottom."
  callouts={[
    { box: "developer", label: "Developer: opens on the chat with the Memory panel. Security stays out of your way until something is blocked." },

    { box: "manager", label: "Manager: leads with spending, savings and how much work gets done." },

    { box: "lucid-agent", label: "LUCID Agent: a hands-free view where you talk with LUCID by voice and the side panels step away." },

/>

<Steps>

1. Read the five cards. Each says where it lands and what you see there.

2. Click the card that fits you best, or click **Skip - use Developer**.

</Steps>

<Aside>
If you pick **LUCID Agent**, the side panels step away and the LUCID stage fills the window. Press **Esc** to step out to the full app. To step back in, click **Stage** at the top of the window.
</Aside>

## Add your work email, or skip it

Next, LUCID asks **Set your work email (optional)**. LUCID uses it to count how many lines of code each AI wrote in each project. It stays on your computer.

<Shot
  id="get-started-work-email"
  alt="The Set your work email (optional) box with an email field, a Skip - use DEMO-PC button and a Save button."
  callouts={[
    { box: "email", label: "Type your work email here if you want code activity counted under your name." },

<Steps>

1. Type your work email and click **Save**, or click the **Skip** button. The Skip button shows your computer's name.

</Steps>

## Answer the government question

LUCID then asks **Do you handle Government CUI?** CUI means Controlled Unclassified Information, a kind of sensitive government data. Most people answer No.

<Shot
  id="get-started-cui-question"
  alt="The Do you handle Government CUI? box with two choices: Yes - set up CUI (gov) mode, and No - standard use."
  callouts={[
    { box: "yes", label: "Yes: only for government users and contractors who must keep CUI on an approved service." },
    { box: "no", label: "No - standard use: for everyone else." },
  ]}
/>

<Steps>

1. Click **No - standard use**, unless your job requires CUI handling.

</Steps>

## Connect an AI, now or later

If no AI is set up yet, LUCID asks **Connect an AI provider**. A [model provider](/glossary/#model-provider) is the company whose AI does the thinking, such as Anthropic for Claude or OpenAI for ChatGPT. LUCID needs at least one before you can chat.

<Shot
  id="get-started-connect-provider"
  alt="The Connect an AI provider box with two choices: Connect a provider, and I'll do it later."
  callouts={[
    { box: "connect", label: "Connect a provider opens the list of AI providers, where you sign in or paste a key." },
    { box: "later", label: "I'll do it later closes the box. You can connect from the model picker or Settings any time." },
  ]}
/>

<Steps>

1. Click **Connect a provider** to sign in or paste a key now, or click **I'll do it later**. You can add one any time from the model picker or Settings.

</Steps>

The [Connect your AI](/connect/) section walks through both ways, step by step.

## Take the short tour, or skip it

Last, a short guided tour points at the main parts of the window, one card at a time. Which parts it shows depends on the role you picked.

<Shot
  id="get-started-tour"

  callouts={[

## Make LUCID yours, or later

A card titled **Make LucidAgent yours** appears in the top-right corner when LUCID first opens, and it stays until you answer it. It offers to bring in your chat history from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, so LUCID can tailor its answers to you. It is optional, private and stays on your computer.

<Shot
  id="get-started-personalize"
  alt="A card titled Make LucidAgent yours with two buttons: Set up personalization and Later."
  callouts={[
    { box: "setup", label: "Set up personalization opens the Personalization settings." },
    { box: "later", label: "Later closes the card." },
  ]}
/>

<Steps>

1. Click **Later** for now. You can set it up any time in **Settings**, under **Workspace & profile**, in **Personalization**.

</Steps>

A model is the specific AI that answers you, such as one version of Claude. Until you connect an AI, LUCID has nothing to chat with and the status bar keeps saying **Loading models**. Connecting one is the next step.

## Change your role later

<Steps>

1. Click **Settings**, the sliders icon at the bottom of the icon bar on the left.

2. Click **Workspace & profile**.

3. Click **Profile** to open it.

4. Under **Role**, click the role you want. It takes effect right away.

</Steps>
