An [agent](/glossary/#agent) is an AI that can take actions, such as reading and editing files. LUCID works in folders on your computer, and on the right rail it shows each folder you work in as an **Agent**: a mini Lucid in a glass brick. Everything an Agent starts works inside its folder.

An Agent lights up while any agent is working in its folder, whether that is the main chat or one of the agents in your [Fleet](/glossary/#fleet).

## Open the Agents view

The right rail is the narrow column on the right edge of the window. It has two views: **Agents** and **Metrics**. On a new install it opens on Agents.

3. To go back to the numbers, click the same button again. Its note is now titled **Metrics** and says "Back to the live metrics".

## Where Agents come from

You do not have to set Agents up. LUCID adds one for:

- the folder the main chat works in;
- folders you opened recently;
- the folder of each agent in your Fleet.

You can also add any folder yourself. See [Add an Agent](/agents/add-an-agent/).

Each Agent is named after its folder. When two folders have the same name, the name shows the folder above it too, such as `alpha/api` and `beta/api`.

## What an Agent shows

- **Working:** while any agent works in its folder, the brick stays bright and its Lucid types.
- **Idle:** its Lucid meditates and the brick rests a little dimmer. Hover the rail to brighten every brick.
- **Turn ended:** when an agent in its folder finishes a turn, a ring plays around the brick in its color.

## Put Agents in your order

New Agents appear at the top. To change the order:

- **Drag** an Agent up or down. The other Agents look at it as it passes, and LUCID keeps your order.
- With the keyboard, click an Agent, then press **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down** to move it.

When there are more Agents than fit, thin arrows appear above and below them. Click an arrow to move three Agents, double-click it to move a whole page, or use your mouse wheel.

## The Agent's module

Hover an Agent to open its module beside the rail. Click the Agent instead to open it with the **Activate** choices already open.

Press **Esc** to close the module.

### Change its color

Click one of the preset colors, or drag the color slider. The brick changes color at once, and LUCID keeps it.

### Rename it

<Steps>

1. Open the Agent's module and click **Rename**.

2. Type the new name.

3. Press **Enter** to save it, or **Esc** to keep the old name.

</Steps>

Renaming changes only the name on the rail. Your folder keeps its name.

### Remove it from the rail

<Steps>

1. Open the Agent's module and click **Remove from rail**.

2. LUCID asks **Remove?** Click **Yes** to remove it, or **Cancel** to keep it.

   <Shot
     id="agents-remove-confirm"
     alt="The Agent's module with the Activate choices open. At the bottom, Remove? is followed by a red Yes button and a Cancel button."
     callouts={[
       { box: "yes", label: "Yes removes the Agent from the rail." },
       { box: "no", label: "Cancel keeps it." },
     ]}
   />

3. A note says **Agent removed**. To bring it back right away, click **Undo** in that note.

</Steps>

Removing an Agent only takes it off the rail. Your folder and its files stay as they are, and a removed folder does not come back on its own. To show it again later, add it with **+ Add agent**.
