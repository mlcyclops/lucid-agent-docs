When an [agent](/glossary/#agent) makes a web page, such as a small game or a site, LUCID loads it out of sight first. If the page shows properly, a **preview chip** appears. Click the chip and the page opens in the **Preview panel**, where you can look at it and try it.

A blank page or one that fails to load never gets a chip.

## The preview chip

The chip looks the same everywhere. While the page loads it says **Preparing preview**, with a small moving page picture. When the page is ready it says **Preview ready** and the page's name, with a tiny picture of the page.

You find it in three places:

- **The main chat:** at the top right of the chat column.
- **A Fleet card:** in the upper right corner of the card's conversation. It floats over the text there and never covers the message box. On a narrow card it shows only the tiny picture.
- **Agent Worlds:** above the agent that made the page.

When more pages are waiting, the chip shows a count such as **+1**.

## Open a page

<Steps>

1. Wait until the chip says **Preview ready**.

2. Click the chip. The Preview panel swings out on the right, on its own tab for that page.

   <Shot
     id="agents-preview-panel"
     alt="The Preview panel on the right half of the window showing a green penalty shootout game. Its tabs read Yours, Agent and Mini Games, with Mini Games selected. Buttons at the top include Reload, Device and Screenshot to chat, and an X at the far right."
     callouts={[
       { box: "panel", label: "The Preview panel, showing the page." },
       { box: "tab", label: "The page's tab, named after the agent that made it." },
       { box: "close", label: "Close the panel." },
     ]}
   />

3. Try the page right in the panel. To make the panel wider or narrower, drag its left edge.

4. Click the **X** at the top right to close the panel.

</Steps>

The page stays on its tab after you close the panel. To dismiss the main chat's chip without opening the page, click the small **x** beside it. Its note explains that a page that rendered stays on its Preview tab, and that you can open the panel from the rail any time.

## Open a page from Agent Worlds

In Agent Worlds, each agent that made a page has the chip above it. Main, the main chat, has one too when it made a page.

<Shot
  id="agents-preview-worlds"
  alt="Agent Worlds. Above one agent's glass box is a chip that says Preview ready, Penalty Shootout. The agent's floating Agent, labeled MINI GAMES, stands to the left."
  callouts={[{ box: "chip", label: "The agent's preview chip." }]}
/>

<Steps>

1. Hover the chip. A card shows the page's title and where it is, an **Open preview** button, and the agent's recent snapshots of its pages, if it has any.

2. Click the chip, **Open preview**, or a snapshot. The Preview panel flies out over Agent Worlds, which keeps running behind it.

3. To slide the panel back, click its **X**, press **Esc**, or click the world. Press **Esc** again to close Agent Worlds.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
In Agent Worlds a floating Agent can stand over the chip. Drag the floating Agent aside. See [Floating Agents](/agents/floating-agents/#move-it-or-put-it-back).
</Aside>
