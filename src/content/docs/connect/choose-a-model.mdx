A model is one specific AI that answers you, such as one version of Claude. Each [model provider](/glossary/#model-provider) you connect adds its models to LUCID. The model button at the top of the window shows the model in use, and lets you pick another.

## Pick a model

<Steps>

1. Click the model button at the top of the window. It shows the name of the current model.

   A list opens. **Model** at the top names the model in use.

   <Shot

2. Find the model you want. Models are grouped by family. Type part of a name in the search box to filter the list.

   Each row shows the model's context window, which is how much text it can keep in mind at once, and one to five stars for how capable it is.

3. Click the model. The list closes and the model button shows the new name.

     id="connect-model-chosen"

     callouts={[{ box: "pill", label: "The model button shows the model you picked." }]}
   />

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
To keep a model at the top of the list, hover its row and click the star at the end. It moves to a **Favorites** group. Click the star again to remove it.
</Aside>

No models in the list? Nothing is connected yet. The list says **No models yet. Connect a provider, then Refresh.** Follow [Connect your AI](/connect/) first.

## Mode and thinking

Under the model list are two more settings:

- **Mode:** **Agent** edits files and runs tools on its own. **Ask** asks you to approve each tool call before it runs. **Plan** only reads and drafts a plan, and makes no changes. See [approval](/glossary/#approval).
- **Thinking:** how hard the model thinks before it answers, from **Off** (fastest) to **X-High** (deepest). **Auto** lets the model choose.

## The Login row

Under the model name is a **Login** button. It lists every provider you can sign in to with a subscription, and shows each one's state:

- **Login:** not signed in yet. Click it to sign in. See [Sign in with a subscription](/connect/sign-in-with-a-subscription/).
- **Signed in:** this provider is connected.
- **Log in again:** this sign-in ended. Click it to sign in again.

<Steps>

1. Click the model button.

2. Click **Login**. The list of providers opens. When nothing is connected yet, it is already open.

## When a sign-in ends

A provider can end a saved sign-in, for example after an update. LUCID then tells you:

- The dot on the model button turns amber.
- A notice under the model button says, for example, **Log in to Claude again**. Click **Log in** to sign in again, or **Not now** to hide the notice until LUCID next opens.
- At the top of the Login row, an amber **Log in to Claude again** button appears.

When you log in again, LUCID puts you back on the model you used before the sign-in ended, if that model is still offered.
