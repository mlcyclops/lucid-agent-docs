An [agent](/glossary/#agent) is an AI that takes actions for you, such as reading files or running commands. LUCID has a view called **Visualize** that draws your agents as characters in a 3D scene, with the work flowing between them as streams of light. The main chat is in the middle, and each agent of your [Fleet](/glossary/#fleet) stands around it.

A **Lucid Agent World** turns that scene into a themed setting, such as a snowy workshop, a soccer stadium or a hospital. Each world brings its own cast, stages, sky, three throwables and a storm. Your agents become its characters, so you can see at a glance who is busy, who is waiting and who needs you.

A world only changes how your agents look. It never runs tools, and every agent still sits behind the same LUCID security gate.

LUCID also has two built-in styles that need no world: **Anime** and **Professional**. Everything on these pages works in them too, except the cast choices, which only worlds use.
