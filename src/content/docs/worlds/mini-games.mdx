Each world comes with up to three **mini game** ideas. Many come with a ready-made **sample** you can play right away. You can also ask an agent to build the game: its subagents build it on a glowing scaffold beside it, and the finished game appears as a small button over the agent.

Every game, sample or agent-built, is treated as untrusted. Before it can run, LUCID's security gate checks it. A game the gate blocks is never shown, and the reason is recorded, the same way a blocked tool call is [quarantined](/glossary/#quarantine). A game that passes runs in a [sandbox](/glossary/#sandbox): it has no internet, cannot save anything, and cannot reach LUCID, your files or your keys.

Without a world, Visualize offers two games of its own: **Target tap game** and **Memory cards game**.

<Aside>
The pictures on this page come from LUCID's built-in demo team in the Christmas world, so they say **DEMO**. In the demo, **Build** is switched off, because the demo agents are not real.
</Aside>

## Play a sample

<Steps>

1. In Visualize, click an agent's figure, or Main in the middle. Its panel opens.

2. Under **Mini game**, find the game, and click **Play sample**.

## Have an agent build a game

<Steps>

1. Click a working agent's figure, or Main.

2. Under **Mini game**, click **Build** next to the game you want. LUCID sends the agent the game's instructions as an ordinary task. If the agent is busy, the task waits until its current turn ends.

3. Watch the scaffold rise beside the agent as its subagents work. A finished step locks in with a flash. A failed step flickers red.

4. When the agent finishes, a small button with the game's name appears over it. Click it. The same game card opens, and the game goes through the same security gate and the same sandbox as a sample.

</Steps>

If the gate blocks the game, the card says **Not shown**, followed by the reason, and the game never runs.
