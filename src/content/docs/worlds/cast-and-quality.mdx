The **cast** is the set of characters a world draws for your agents. By default, LUCID gives the team a mixed look: different skin tones, hair and presentation, and each agent keeps its look as others come and go. You can change that for the whole team or for one agent.

**Quality** sets how much detail Visualize draws. It applies to the built-in styles and to every world.

<Aside>
The cast choices appear only while a world is in use. The built-in **Anime** and **Professional** styles have their own characters and do not use them.
</Aside>

## Choose the look of the whole team

<Steps>

1. Open Visualize in a world. See [Open Agent Worlds](/worlds/open-agent-worlds/).

2. Click **View** at the top, and scroll down to **Cast**.

3. Click a skin shade, a hair color or a hair style. Visualize switches to **Same for all** and redraws every character with your choice.

## Set the quality

<Steps>

1. Click **View** at the top of Visualize.

2. Under **Quality**, click the button. Each click moves to the next setting, in this order: **Auto**, **Full**, **Reduced**, **Minimal**, **Ultra**, **Cinematic**. LUCID confirms each change with a short notice.

   <Shot
     id="worlds-view-menu"
     alt="The View menu. Under Quality, a button reads Auto · ultra (Radeon RX 580 Series)."
     caption="Auto names the level it chose and the graphics card it found. This picture was taken on a computer with a Radeon RX 580."
     callouts={[
       { box: "quality", label: "The Quality button." },
       { box: "layout", label: "Layout." },
       { box: "fly", label: "Fly to approvals." },
     ]}
   />

</Steps>

What each setting does:

- **Auto** picks for you, from your graphics card, your battery and your processor. A computer with a separate graphics card that is plugged in gets **Ultra**. A computer without real graphics support gets **Minimal**. If the scene cannot keep up, Auto steps down a level and tells you. Auto never picks **Cinematic**.
- **Cinematic**: everything in Ultra, plus soft shadowing where objects meet and a camera-style blur of what is out of focus. For strong graphics cards.
- **Ultra**: 12,000 light particles, sharper pixels, smoothed edges, shadows under the figures, and a glow on light worlds too. For computers with a separate graphics card.
- **Full**: 6,000 particles at up to 60 frames a second, with glass effects and glow.
- **Reduced**: 2,500 particles at up to 30 frames a second.
- **Minimal**: 600 particles at up to 20 frames a second, with no lightning.

Pick a fixed level to keep it: Auto is the only setting that steps down on its own.

<Aside type="tip">
The Knowledge Graph uses the same setting. Changing Quality here changes it there too.
</Aside>
