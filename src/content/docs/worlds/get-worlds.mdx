You get worlds from the LUCID store. You can buy one world at a time, or a [World Pack](/glossary/#world-pack) that unlocks many worlds at once. Each is a one-time purchase, and each card shows its price.

You can shop in the app, or on the web at [store.lucid-agents.com](https://store.lucid-agents.com/). Either way, the purchase is kept on the Google account you sign in with, so you can install it on every computer where you sign in.

## Open the store

<Steps>

1. Open Visualize, click **Worlds**, then click **Get more worlds**. Visualize closes and the **Plugins** store opens on its **Premium** tab.

   Two other ways in: the **Plugins** button at the top of the Preview panel, or the command list (**Ctrl+K**, or **⌘K** on a Mac) and **Open Plugins**.

2. The top of the Premium tab lists the World Packs.

## Buy a world

<Steps>

1. On the world's card, click **Sign in to buy** if you see it. LUCID opens a sign-in page in your web browser. Sign in with your Google account, then come back to LUCID. The card now says **Buy**, followed by the price.

2. Click **Buy**. A box shows what you get and a summary of the license.

3. Tick **I accept the LUCID Premium Plugin License**, then click **Continue to checkout**.

4. Checkout opens in your web browser. Pay there, then come back to LUCID.

   The card says **Finish checkout in your browser. The install starts when you come back to LUCID.** When you return, LUCID installs the world.

</Steps>

## Buy a World Pack

A pack installs nothing by itself. It unlocks worlds on your account, and each unlocked world then installs from its own card.

- **Patron Class**: click **Buy**, accept the license and check out. Every world is unlocked.
- **Sponsor**: click **Buy** and pick exactly 6 worlds. **Continue** turns on when 6 are picked. Then accept the license and check out. Your 6 worlds are unlocked, and you also get world credits. A world you do not own then shows **Use a credit** instead of **Buy**.

After checkout, LUCID says **Unlocked. Install your worlds from their cards.**

## Install a world

<Steps>

1. On an unlocked world's card, click **Install**.

2. LUCID downloads the world and checks it. The card says **Downloading the signed package**, then **Checking the signature, checksums, and security scan**. A world that fails a check is not installed, and LUCID says why.

3. When it is done, LUCID says the world is installed. Click **Open in Visualize** in that notice, or on the card, to see it.

</Steps>

<Aside>
A world opened from the store can show the built-in style for a moment before the world appears.
</Aside>

## Keep your worlds

An upgrade of LUCID does not remove your worlds. LUCID also keeps a signed spare copy of every world you install.

Each time LUCID starts, it checks your worlds. If one is missing or damaged, LUCID puts it back from the spare copy, after checking its signature, checksums and security scan again. It then tells you once, for example **Repaired 1 Lucid Agent World after an update**.

If the spare copy cannot fix it, LUCID reinstalls the world from the store, as long as your account owns it. If you are signed out, LUCID asks you to sign in and offers **Sign in** and **Retry**.

You can also run this check yourself:

<Steps>

1. In the store, click the **Installed** tab.

2. Click **Restore purchases**. LUCID checks every world and store plugin on this computer and reinstalls any that is missing or damaged.

## Move a world to a computer with no internet

<Steps>

1. On the world's card, click **Export**. LUCID saves the world's signed file.

2. Copy the file to the other computer.

3. There, open Visualize, click **Worlds**, then **Import world file...**, and pick the file. LUCID checks the signature, the contents and the security scan again before it installs it.

</Steps>

The world still needs your purchase to show. A world is also kept usable for a while on a computer that cannot reach the store, so a computer that goes offline keeps working.
