Agent Worlds live in a view called **Visualize**. It shows the main chat, which LUCID calls **Main**, in the middle, and each [agent](/glossary/#agent) of your [Fleet](/glossary/#fleet) around it. Small figures near an agent are its **subagents**: helpers it starts for part of a task.

Opening Visualize never changes what your agents are doing. It only watches them.

<Aside>
The pictures on this page come from a demo team built into LUCID for testing. That is why they say **DEMO**. Your own view shows your own agents.
</Aside>

## Look around

**In 3D**, drag to turn the scene, right-drag to slide it, and scroll to zoom.

**In 2D**, you see the scene from above, like a map. Drag to slide it and scroll to zoom.

**Auto camera** moves the camera for you. It glides to the agent working hardest. As soon as you drag, slide or zoom, Auto camera switches off so you keep control. Tick it again to hand the camera back.

**Fit** (the target button) brings every figure back into view. It also switches Auto camera off.

Under **View**, **Layout** offers two arrangements: **Orbit** puts the agents on a ring around Main, and **Constellation** spreads them around Main in 3D.

## Talk to an agent

Click a figure to open its panel beside it. The panel shows what the agent did last, buttons such as **Stop turn**, **End agent** and **Kill**, and a message box. Type there to send that agent a message. Press **Esc**, or click an empty spot, to close the panel.

## Close Visualize

Click the **X** at the top right, or press **Esc**. Your agents keep working.
